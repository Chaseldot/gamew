# Art Assets Showcase Design

## Goal

Use the new `art-assets/` library as the public-facing art showcase, while reducing the top navigation to the five pages that matter for first-time browsing: Home, World, Story, Systems, and Art.

## Approved Direction

The top navigation becomes:

- `首页`
- `世界`
- `故事`
- `系统`
- `美术`

Existing child pages remain in the site and can still be reached from home cards or content links, but they no longer compete for space in the global header.

## Approach

The site stays a static HTML/CSS/JS showcase under `docs/reference/website-blueprint/site`. Structured content remains the source of truth in `docs/reference/website-blueprint/content/12-website-assets/web_export.json`; `site/data.js` remains generated by `scripts/build-site-data.mjs`.

The art page will become the asset-library gateway:

- Feature chapter maps, chapter key art, location concepts, scenes, combat images, and character exploration.
- Read from copied web assets under `site/assets/art/`, with item metadata in the structured export.
- Add filters that match the new library taxonomy instead of the older small gallery.
- Keep the lightbox interaction and static hosting model.

## Constraints

- Do not remove existing child pages.
- Do not overwrite unrelated working-tree edits.
- Keep image filenames Chinese and preserve 4K landscape checks for concept, scene, and combat artwork.
- Keep the generated-file workflow intact.
